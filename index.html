About

Sabrina Polio, LCSW

After more than a decade’s experience in the field, Sabrina recognized that mental health services seemed to be focused more on mental illness than mental health.

Determined to change the narrative and offer a more empowering path to clients, Sabrina founded Brighter Side Therapy. I believe in the power of connection and authenticity in building resilience. I strive to create an open and welcoming environment that empowers my clients to explore, learn, and embrace their own life stories.

Services

My Specialties

Individual Therapy

I work primarily with teenagers and adults in one-on-one therapy. I utilize somatic work and an Internal Family Systems (IFS) framework to support clients in their mental health journey. I work with a number of presenting problems including anxiety, depression, and athlete mental health.

Sessions can be conducted in person in the Old Town office or virtually.

Book a consultation

Sports Team Consultation

I offer workshops and consultation to sports teams, coaches, parents, and educators. This consultation can help to improve relationships between coaches and parents and their athletes as well as strengthening team dynamics and athletic performance. If you are interested in these services, please reach out to learn more and discuss how we can curate a unique experience for your needs.

Sessions can be conducted in person in the Old Town office or virtually.

Ask about workshops

Parenting Support

I offer parenting support either in addition to individual therapy with an adolescent or as a separate service for parents looking to improve their skills and relationship to their child.

Sessions can be conducted in person in the Old Town office or virtually.

Athlete mental health

A passion for working with athletes

As a former high school and collegiate rower, I understand the physical, emotional, and mental challenges of athletes and their families. I have experience as an NCAA Division I rower at Yale and a deep respect and passion for supporting athletes and their families.

I work with athletes of all ages to reach their competitive goals while maintaining a solid mental and emotional grounding for longterm success.
